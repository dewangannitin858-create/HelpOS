# HelpOS

**HelpOS** is an upgraded, **100% local-first** student productivity workspace forked from [Arshhabh/StudyOS](https://github.com/Arshhabh/StudyOS). It combines everyday academic organization with a built-in **Attendance Calendar** so students can track daily lectures, monitor attendance percentages, and manage their semester—all stored privately on their own device without requiring cloud accounts.

---

## Who Is This Useful For?

- **College & University Students:** Especially useful for students who need to maintain strict attendance criteria (e.g., 75% rule) and track subject-wise attendance daily.
- **Privacy-First & Offline Learners:** Works **100% locally** in your browser (`localStorage`)—no mandatory sign-ups, subscriptions, or cloud databases.
- **All-in-One Organizers:** Ideal for students balancing assignments, exams, tasks, notes, focus sessions, habits, and daily class schedules in one workspace.

---

## Features

### Added in HelpOS
- **Attendance Calendar (`Academics → Attendance`):**
  - **Custom Semester & Timetable Setup:** Set your semester name, target attendance percentage (default 75%), add/remove subjects, and build your weekly lecture timetable (Mon–Sat).
  - **Daily Lecture Toggles:** Click any date on the calendar and mark each scheduled lecture as **ON (Present)**, **OFF (Absent)**, or **Holiday (Cancelled)**.
  - **Live Percentage Tracker:** Automatically calculates overall and subject-wise attendance percentages with safe/danger indicators.
  - **100% Local Storage:** Attendance data is saved privately on your device (`helpos.attendance.v1`).

### Core StudyOS Workspace Features
- **Dashboard:** Quick overview of your study progress and daily priorities.
- **Deadline Calendar:** Track tasks, assignments, and exam dates in Day/Week/Month views.
- **Tasks, Assignments & Exams:** Stay on top of coursework and upcoming tests.
- **Notes & Goals:** Keep study notes organized and track long-term academic goals.
- **Focus Timer & Habits:** Built-in productivity timer and daily habit tracker.
- **Analytics & Utilities:** Visual insights into your study consistency and handy student tools.

### Coming Soon
- **Aptitude Test Module:** Local practice tests and score tracking for placement and exam prep.

## How It Works

HelpOS runs directly on your system and stores all data locally in your browser's `localStorage`:
1. Use the left sidebar to switch between study tools.
2. Open **Academics → Attendance** and click **Edit Semester & Timetable** to add your subjects and weekly schedule.
3. Return to **Calendar & Daily**, select any date, and toggle each lecture **ON**, **OFF**, or **Holiday**.
4. Your overall and subject-wise attendance percentages update instantly.
